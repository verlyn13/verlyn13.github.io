## Selected work

Independent, unpaid projects developed alongside my faculty appointment. The code and case studies show what I built, how I investigated failures, and what the evidence establishes.

- **Host Capability Substrate** TypeScript prototype for defining what agents may do and recording evidence behind authorization decisions. Public schemas and automated architecture checks make the design inspectable. [Examine the public implementation →](/projects/host-capability-substrate.html)
- **ScopeCam** Android microscope application spanning Kotlin, Compose, C++, JNI, and USB camera integration. Built capture and media workflows and diagnosed a camera-replug deadlock across the managed/native boundary. Signed client alpha delivered in June 2026; source is private and recovery retains a resource-leak tradeoff after timeout. [Read the native engineering case study →](/projects/scopecam.html)
- **Budget Triage** Financial evidence workbench with exact monetary handling, provenance, tenant isolation, and human review. A public source snapshot is available; active development is private; the system is not deployed. [Review the workbench →](/projects/budget-triage.html)

[Explore the method and evidence →](https://github.com/verlyn13/eval-lab-methodology)
- **Dicee** Live multiplayer browser game using Rust/WebAssembly, SvelteKit, Cloudflare Durable Objects, and WebSockets, with public source and a live deployment. [Review the delivered system →](/projects/dicee.html)
