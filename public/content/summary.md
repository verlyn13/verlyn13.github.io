# Jeffrey V. Johnson, Ph.D.

**Building infrastructure for model evaluation and agent systems**

I build software and development infrastructure for reliable agent-assisted
work: clear specifications, explicit permissions, reproducible environments,
automated checks, and evidence that can be reviewed.

My work includes Python verification tools, an Android microscope application,
TypeScript data systems, and deployed web software. I define tasks, guide and
review agent-assisted implementation, investigate failures, and check whether
the evidence supports the result.

**Seeking research engineering and systems work with meaningful ownership on
a specialized team.**

I am a tenured associate professor of mathematics at Kenai Peninsula
College/UAA. My record includes mathematical research, statistical analysis for
a published study of 1,593 children and adolescents, university teaching in the
United States and Egypt, and Peace Corps service in South Africa. The engineering
projects are independent, unpaid work developed alongside that faculty appointment.
